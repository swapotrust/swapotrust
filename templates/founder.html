Kapil Agrawal founded SWAPO Trust in Hathras on a belief that runs through all of its work: seva is a form of worship, and a duty we owe one another.

The name he chose, Social Welfare and Peace Organisation, puts two ideas side by side. The trust deed covers a wide field, from health and education to the protection of animals and the environment, from yoga and satsang to helping people whose rights have been violated.

The work SWAPO is best known for began under his leadership on 16 July 2016, when the Trust handed its first monthly ration to poor MDR-TB patients at the District TB Centre. It has continued every month since.

As National President he leads a team of members called SKY, SWAPO Karm Yogis, with branches in Hathras, Mathura and Surat. He also takes public problems to the government, through letters and memoranda to ministers and the Chief Minister.
