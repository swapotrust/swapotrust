SWAPO Trust's new website, and with it **SWAPO Times**, is now live. From today, every activity of the Trust – nutrition for TB patients, drinking-water seva, health camps, blanket distribution and more – will be published here with photographs.

Each month's stories will also be collected automatically into an **e-paper** that anyone can download and share on WhatsApp.

Join this journey of seva – as a volunteer, a member or a partner.
