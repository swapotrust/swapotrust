SWAPO, the Social Welfare and Peace Organisation, was founded in Hathras by Kapil Agrawal. The idea behind it is simple and old. Seva is a form of worship, and spiritual values mean little unless they turn into help for someone who needs it.

The Trust is registered as a public charitable trust with its head office at City Plaza, Sadabad Gate, Hathras. Its trust deed gives it a wide brief: humanitarian service, health, education, the environment, the protection of all living beings, and the removal of harmful customs and casteism.

In practice, SWAPO is a group of traders, doctors, teachers, professionals and students from Hathras who give their time and money every month. We call each of them a SKY, a SWAPO Karm Yogi. Today the Trust also has branches in Mathura and Surat.
